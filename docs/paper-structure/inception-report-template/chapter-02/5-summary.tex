\section{Summary}
\label{sec_chapter2_summary}

This chapter has surveyed close to thirty papers relevant to autonomous, LLM-driven penetration testing, covering single-agent systems, multi-agent orchestration, planning-augmented agents, multi-host red teaming, API/GraphQL-specific testing, and the benchmark datasets used to evaluate them. A comparative reading of this literature points, tentatively, to a recurring split between systems optimized for broad exploration and systems optimized for dependency-aware reasoning, with few if any surveyed systems combining both, and few evaluated across more than one attack-surface family.

As this report reflects an early stage of the thesis, the survey above should be read as a snapshot of work completed so far rather than a closed literature review. Over the coming months, the review will be deepened -- in particular around the planning-augmented and memory-augmented systems, where the fewest papers were found -- and the observations in Section~\ref{sec_research_gap} will be used to motivate a more precisely scoped research question and methodology in the chapters that follow.
