\section{Organization}
\label{sec:intro_organization}

This report reflects the first stage of work on this thesis, and its organisation is intended as a plan for the full document we expect to submit in approximately six months, not a description of chapters that already exist in complete form. Chapter~\ref{chapter_backStudy} presents a review of a focused selection of papers on autonomous VAPT, organised by architectural approach, and identifies the gaps in prior work that motivated the direction described in Sections~\ref{sec:intro_problem} and~\ref{sec:intro_objectives}; this chapter is the most developed at present. Chapter~\ref{chapter_proposedMethod} will present our proposed system design as it develops --- currently sketched at a high level and expected to be substantially revised as implementation proceeds. Chapter~\ref{chapter_implementations} will lay out our work breakdown, timeline, and milestones for the remaining months, and will be kept current as work progresses. Chapter~\ref{chapter_experimentalResults} will specify and, eventually, report on our evaluation plan once an implementation exists to evaluate. Chapter~\ref{chapter_conclusion} will summarise the completed work, and is not expected to be written in any substantive form until much later in the thesis timeline.
