\section{Organization}
\label{sec:intro_organization}

This report reflects roughly the first month of work on this thesis, and its organization is intended as a plan for the full document we expect to submit in approximately six months, not a description of chapters that already exist in complete form. Chapter~\ref{chapter_backStudy} presents our survey of the 29-paper corpus underlying this work, organized by architectural pattern, and the gaps in prior work that motivated the direction described in Sections~\ref{sec:intro_problem} and~\ref{sec:intro_objectives}; this chapter is the most complete at present. Chapter~\ref{chapter_proposedMethod} will present our system design as it develops -- currently sketched at a high level, but expected to be substantially revised as implementation proceeds. Chapter~\ref{chapter_implementations} will lay out our work breakdown, timeline, and milestones for the remaining months, and will be kept up to date as work progresses. Chapter~\ref{chapter_experimentalResults} will specify and, eventually, report on our evaluation plan once an implementation exists to evaluate. Chapter~\ref{chapter_conclusion} will summarize the completed work, and is not expected to be written in any substantive form until much later in the thesis timeline.
