\section{Organization}
\label{sec:intro_organization}

This report reflects the first stage of work on this thesis; its organization serves as a plan for the full document to be submitted over the remaining thesis period, not a description of chapters already complete.

Chapter~\ref{chapter_backStudy} reviews eleven papers directly relevant to autonomous LLM-based penetration testing, organised by contribution category across single-agent systems, multi-agent orchestration, planning-augmented approaches, non-web attack surfaces, and the two benchmark studies --- CVE-Bench \citep{zhu2025cvebench} and PentestEval \citep{yang2025pentesteval} --- that provide the quantitative failure-mode evidence motivating this thesis. A comparative analysis closes the chapter with the research gap that Sections~\ref{sec:intro_problem} and~\ref{sec:intro_objectives} translate into concrete research questions.

Chapter~\ref{chapter_proposedMethod} presents the proposed RedGrid architecture under active specification: a four-layer orchestration hierarchy, a Dual-Layer World Model separating confirmed evidence from inferred hypotheses, a Vulnerability Dependency Graph (VDG) with UCB-guided node selection, and a Cross-Mission Memory module. This chapter is subject to revision as implementation proceeds and early experiments return feedback.

Chapter~\ref{chapter_implementations} lays out the work breakdown, timeline, and milestones for the remaining months of the thesis, and will be updated as work progresses to reflect completed and deferred items.

Chapter~\ref{chapter_experimentalResults} specifies the evaluation plan and will report results against CVE-Bench, PentestEval, PrediQL, and Incalmo MHBench once an implementation exists; per-surface reporting and baseline comparisons are described in advance of any results being available.

Chapter~\ref{chapter_conclusion} summarises the completed work, states which hypotheses the results support or refute, and identifies directions for future work; that chapter takes substantive form only once the evaluation is complete.
