\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec_conclusion_methodologies}

This chapter provided a detailed overview of the proposed methodologies for DataLex, an innovative integration of SIEM and Generative AI technologies aimed at improving cybersecurity monitoring and response. By combining the strengths of traditional SIEM components such as Wazuh, Suricata, and Elasticsearch with the power of AI-driven analysis using LLAMA 3.0 and DeepSeek Qwen, DataLex enhances threat detection, reduces response time, and provides explainable insights for security teams.

DataLex's unique features, including chat-based natural language querying and its offline, privacy-preserving architecture, position it as a next-generation SIEM solution that addresses the limitations of traditional systems. The system's ability to provide contextual, real-time analysis and its adaptability to emerging threats make it a valuable tool for modern cybersecurity operations.

The next chapters evaluate the system's performance in real-world scenarios, comparing its effectiveness to traditional SIEM solutions, and explore its potential for broader adoption in various industries.
