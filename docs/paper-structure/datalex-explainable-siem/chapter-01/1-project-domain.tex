\section{Project Domain and Social Significance}
\label{sec_project_domain}

Traditional security measures are no longer sufficient due to the increase in sophisticated cyber threats brought about by the growth of digital infrastructure. Modern cybersecurity relies heavily on Security Information and Event Management (SIEM), which makes it possible to monitor and respond to security problems in real time. A sophisticated Generative AI-driven SIEM solution like DataLex is now necessary for proactive threat detection and intelligent threat analysis due to the growing complexity of cloud computing, IoT, and distributed networks.

This project improves cybersecurity resilience by protecting important digital assets and sensitive data. DataLex offers contextualized threat insights through the integration of Generative AI into SIEM, facilitating automated, human-like threat assessments that increase the effectiveness of incident response. This method makes security intelligence more widely available while lowering dependency on specialized security experts. Increasing cyber defenses helps businesses avoid financial losses, promotes trust in online transactions, and guarantees adherence to international cybersecurity laws.

\paragraph{Emerging Application Areas:}
The potential applications of enhanced SIEM solutions go far beyond typical enterprise cybersecurity. Emerging domains requiring sophisticated threat identification and analysis include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Critical Infrastructure Protection}: Protecting national power grids, transportation systems, healthcare networks, and telecommunications infrastructure from potential cyber-physical attacks.
    \item \textbf{Financial Technology (FinTech) Security}: Securing complicated financial transaction systems, blockchain networks, and digital payment platforms against sophisticated financial crime and cyber espionage.
    \item \textbf{Healthcare Cyber-security}: Ensuring the integrity and confidentiality of sensitive medical records, safeguarding medical devices, and adhering to strict data protection standards.
    \item \textbf{Smart City Ecosystems}: Monitoring and protecting integrated urban infrastructure, such as intelligent transportation systems, municipal services, and public utility networks.
    \item \textbf{Advanced Research and Academic Institutions}: Defending sensitive research data, intellectual property, and collaborative research environments against targeted cyber-attacks.
\end{itemize}
