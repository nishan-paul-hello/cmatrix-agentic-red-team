\section{Research Objectives}
\label{sec_research_objectives}

Strategic research requires clear, defined goals that drive innovation and address critical technological gaps. This section outlines the precise objectives of the DataLex project, providing a comprehensive roadmap for developing an advanced, privacy-preserving security intelligence system. The key research aims of the DataLex project are:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Architectural Innovation}: Creating a full SIEM system that utilizes offline generative AI capabilities.
    \item \textbf{Privacy Enhancement}: Showcasing a threat detection mechanism that ensures entire data sovereignty.
    \item \textbf{Explainability Development}: Developing an AI-powered security analysis approach with strong interpretability.
    \item \textbf{Performance Evaluation}: Thoroughly evaluating the proposed system's efficiency, accuracy, and comparative advantage.
    \item \textbf{Scalability Validation}: Demonstration of the system's adaptability to different organizational contexts.
\end{itemize}
