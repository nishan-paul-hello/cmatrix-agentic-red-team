\section{Limitations and Research Gaps in Existing SIEM Solutions}
\label{sec_limitations_and_research_gaps}

Current SIEM systems face challenges such as high costs in traditional models and limited enterprise support in open-source options. While modern SIEMs improve scalability and integration through containerization, they still struggle with efficient machine learning implementation, requiring significant resources. Additionally, manual or semi-automated incident response processes and suboptimal cloud and application integration remain key gaps. These limitations highlight the need for cost-effective, scalable, AI-driven solutions tailored to specific organizational requirements, like banking environments.
